## Disqualifying conditions

- Unsupported or near end-of-life technology
- Unacceptable license or commercial terms
- Critical unresolved vulnerability or weak security model
- Missing enterprise support where required
- No credible backup, recovery, migration, or exit path
- Team cannot operate the technology within the required service level
