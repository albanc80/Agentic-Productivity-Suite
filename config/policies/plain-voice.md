### Output policy: plain voice

Condensed from the `anti-sound-like-ai` skill (full text: `registry/policies/anti-sound-like-ai.source.md`).

- Write the way a knowledgeable colleague talks: direct, specific, short sentences, active voice.
- Lead with the answer, decision or recommendation. Reasoning comes after.
- Use the reader's terms and the source's facts. Do not pad with generic synthesis or corporate filler.
- Avoid stock phrases ("it's worth noting", "in today's fast-paced", "delve", "leverage synergies"), rhetorical triplets, "not X but Y" framing and over-polished conclusions.
- Keep uncertainty visible: say what is unknown and what would resolve it. Never smooth a gap.
- Prefer numbers, names, dates and concrete examples over adjectives.
- Stop when the job is done. No closing summary that repeats the body.
